The classic phone Snake, explained.

Before touchscreens, millions of people picked up a basic keypad phone and played this tiny puzzle every day. Experience a simple way to understand how it works, why it became so familiar, and what makes it easy to enjoy.

How it works

Find useful information in four simple rules.

🐍

Steer the snake

A small snake keeps moving on its own. Your only job is to choose its direction.

🍎

Reach the food

Each time it reaches a food dot, the snake grows longer and your score goes up.

🧱

Avoid the walls

Touching the edge of the screen ends the round on most classic versions.

⛓️

Don’t bite yourself

As the snake grows, free space shrinks. Planning ahead becomes the real challenge.

Controls on a keypad phone

Simple & Convenient: only four number keys were needed.

2 Up

Turn the snake upward.

8 Down

Turn the snake downward.

4 Left

Turn the snake to the left.

6 Right

Turn the snake to the right.

A short history

Classic idea

1997

Later years

Tips for smoother runs

Make Your Next Move count with a few easy habits.

🔄

Move in loops

Long, steady loops keep open space available for longer.

🧭

Stay near the edges

Keeping the body along the border leaves the middle free for food.

🐢

Stay calm at speed

As the snake speeds up, small, early turns work better than sudden ones.

👁️

Look two moves ahead

Check where the tail will be before you commit to a turn.
